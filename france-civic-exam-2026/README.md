## What the post covers
The examen civique and A2/B1 French requirement that has applied since 1 January 2026 to a **first** carte de séjour pluriannuelle (A2 + CSP exam) or a **first** carte de résident / RLD-UE (B1 + CR exam). It explains that **renewing an existing CSP or CR is not concerned**, and what "first" really means (CST → CSP, CSP → CR). It covers exemptions (over 65, international protection, stateless, non-CIR titles under CESEDA L413-5 such as students and talent passports, ≥1 academic year of higher education in France, Algeria/Tunisia accords), accepted French proofs (TCF/TEF IRN valid 2 years; DELF/DALF/DUEF/DFP/DCL; French diplomas level 3+ / RNCP / brevet; foreign diplomas refused), exam format and rules, centres, fees since 1 May 2026, filing timing, a checklist and FAQ.

## Brief facts: verified vs corrected
**Verified:** exam before filing a first CSP/CR on ANEF; 40 MCQs in French (28 knowledge + 12 situations), 45 min, pass 32/40; approved centres (CCIP + France Éducation international networks); over-65 exempt; certain card types exempt; A2 for first CSP, B1 for first CR; renewals exempt; applies to cards whose validity starts on/after 1 Jan 2026 (Haute-Vienne prefecture); fees since 1 May 2026: first issue €350 (€300 tax + €50 stamp), renewal €250, reduced renewal €100, late renewal +€180.

**Added / corrected:**
- **Reduced first issue is €150** (was €75) for students, interns, au pairs, job-seekers/business creators, family reunification and seasonal workers. The brief left this out. (A18881)
- "Reduced €100" applies to **renewals** only. (A18881)
- **Exemptions are wider than "certain card types":** besides protection beneficiaries and stateless people, every title outside the CIR (CESEDA L413-5: students, interns, au pairs, temporary/seasonal workers, talent passports < 10 years…) plus people with ≥3 years of French secondary school or ≥1 academic year of higher education in France are exempt for a CSP (Bas-Rhin). For a **first CR / RLD-UE**, B1 + CR exam still applies even to former talent / Blue Card holders and protection beneficiaries. (F39530, Bas-Rhin)
- **Language proof is narrower than "TCF IRN/TCF, DELF etc.":** only TCF (FEI) and TEF (CCIP), 4 skills in one in-person session, valid 2 years; or DELF/DALF/DELF Pro/DUEF/DFP/DCL (no expiry); or a French State diploma level 3+, RNCP level 3+, or brevet (France or abroad). Diplomas issued by foreign authorities are refused, even from francophone countries. (arrêté 22 Dec 2025, F34501, Seine-Maritime)
- A CR exam pass is also valid for a CSP; the certificate never expires; unlimited retakes; no negative marking; fraud = 2-year ban. (Décret 2025-647, arrêté 10 Oct 2025, Ministère de l'Intérieur)

## Uncertainties: please check before publishing
1. **"Validity starts on/after 1 Jan 2026, whatever the filing date"** comes only from the Haute-Vienne prefecture page. I couldn't find it in the décret. The text attributes it to that prefecture.
2. **Fee for CST → CSP or CSP → CR:** the Oise prefecture (19 Mar 2026) says change-of-category renewals pay the renewal tax (€250), while Service-Public F35799 shows €350 for a CSP. The post tells readers to pay what ANEF asks for. Worth a word with the Bas-Rhin prefecture if you get the chance.
3. **Former students (≥1 academic year in French higher education) moving to a salarié CSP:** the post says they are usually exempt, based on the Bas-Rhin L413-5 list. It advises keeping enrolment certificates and checking with the prefecture.
4. **English-taught French master's as B1 proof:** the arrêté doesn't mention the language of instruction. The post flags this and says to ask the prefecture.
5. **TCF taken in Tehran for کمپوس فرانسه:** called "probably not usable" (2-year validity; IRN is the residence version). There's no official statement either way.
6. **DELF taken in Iran:** said to count "in principle" (French State diploma, no restriction on where it was taken in the list), but nothing official confirms this.
7. **Récépissé as ID at the exam:** the arrêté requires a valid titre de séjour. The post advises asking the centre.
8. immigration.interieur.gouv.fr blocked automated fetches (Cloudflare), so its pages were read through search extracts only. The same facts are confirmed on service-public and formation-civique.interieur.gouv.fr.
9. Centre prices and session dates (Stralang €70 civic / €150 TCF IRN; CIEL €155, sessions 3 Nov and 1 Dec 2026) were checked on 5 Oct 2026 and will change. The fa CIEL line cites the 1 Dec 2026 session (deadline 30 Oct). Update it if the post goes live after that.
10. TCF IRN re-marking of written/oral expression is suspended for sessions from 1 Sep 2026 until about autumn 2027 (FEI page, read in the browser).
